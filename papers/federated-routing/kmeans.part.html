The clustering runs live on a proportional subsample of the real client partition, projected to two dimensions with t-SNE so it can be drawn. The deployed router clusters in the sentence encoder's full embedding space, where distances differ, so read this as the shape of the procedure rather than the exact partition it produces. Paper settings are 15 clusters per client and 20 at the server.
