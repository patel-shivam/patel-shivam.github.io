The clustering runs live on a proportional subsample of the real client partition, projected to three dimensions with t-SNE over the same all-mpnet-base-v2 encodings the paper uses. The deployed router clusters in the full 768-dimensional space, where distances differ, so read this as the shape of the procedure rather than the exact partition it produces. Paper settings are 15 clusters per client and 20 at the server.
